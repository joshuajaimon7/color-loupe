Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

ColorLoupe Pro is published by TinyStack Labs. We are committed to building developer and designer tools that respect privacy and execute 100% on-device.

1. On-Device Color Processing

ColorLoupe Pro operates entirely inside your local browser instance. When you pick a pixel color using the EyeDropper API or scan a webpage for computed CSS styles, the color values, RGB calculations, contrast scores, and palette models are processed strictly on your CPU. No pixel data, webpage content, or screen captures are ever recorded, saved remotely, or sent across the network.

2. Local Storage Usage

ColorLoupe Pro uses Chrome's local storage API exclusively on your device to persist:

  • Your most recently picked color value.
  • Your Pro license key and activation state.

This data remains completely local to your browser profile.

3. Payment & Merchant of Record

All license purchases are securely processed by Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record). TinyStack Labs never stores or has access to your credit card, bank details, or payment credentials.

4. Analytics & Tracking

ColorLoupe Pro does not contain third-party trackers, ad scripts, analytics beacons, or remote telemetry.
